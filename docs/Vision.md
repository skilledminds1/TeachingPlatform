# Vision

## Mission

Connect students with great teachers for live online learning — a Preply/AmazingTalker-style marketplace built for educators and learners, starting in South Africa.

## Long-Term Vision

Amazing Skills becomes the default place to find a tutor, book a live video session, and learn — teachers grow their practice; students get quality instruction without friction.

## Product Identity

**We are a live tutoring marketplace** — discovery, booking, video calls, and direct teacher payments.

**We are not an LMS-first product.** Courses, homework, and quizzes are optional future additions, not the core value proposition.

## Core Principles

### 1. Teacher-First Design

Help tutors get discovered, booked, and paid. Every feature answers: *Does this help teachers earn more sessions?*

### 2. Live Learning Is the Product

Video calls are central — not a bolt-on to a course catalog. Booking → payment → video session is the core loop.

### 3. Platform Stays Out of Teacher Payouts

Students pay teachers directly via PayFast or the teacher's PayPal. The platform only collects subscriptions via PayFast.

### 4. Simplicity Over Complexity

Ship the marketplace loop first. Avoid LMS bloat until the core tutoring flow is proven.

### 5. Privacy & Trust

Approved profiles, moderated reviews, reliable sessions. Student and teacher data protected from day one.

## Product Goals

| Goal | Measure |
|------|---------|
| Fast teacher onboarding | Profile submitted within 20 minutes |
| Low friction booking | Book a session in under 5 clicks |
| Reliable video | Session join success rate > 98% |
| Sustainable revenue | MRR from PayFast subscriptions |

## Non-Goals (v1)

- Full LMS / course builder as primary feature
- Platform-handled teacher payouts or escrow
- Enterprise SSO / SAML
- Native mobile apps (responsive web first)
- AI-generated course content
- Commission on session payments

## Success Metrics

- Completed video sessions per month (north star)
- Paying subscribers (Starter+)
- Monthly recurring revenue (MRR) via PayFast
- Marketplace bookings per month
- Net promoter score (NPS)
- Teacher profile approval time (< 48 hours)

## Brand Tone

- **Professional** but approachable
- **Clear** — no jargon
- **Supportive** — feels like a teaching partner, not a corporate portal
